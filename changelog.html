What's new

Every RAMen update, newest first.

14 November 2026

  • License keys work however you paste them: with quotes, a full stop from the email's sentence, dashes a notes app made from the hyphens, or a line break where the email wrapped it.
  • RAMen finds more when you ask in other words: where you live ("I moved to Seattle"), your pets ("a corgi named Waffles"), your work. When nothing matches, your AI gets your newest notes to check, so it can still find the answer.
  • Long lists stay short: "what's left on my list" and loading a big project now fit in one short answer and name the rest.
  • Searching inside a long saved chat also searches its older details.
  • On phones, long words and links wrap on your RAMen page and in the memory panel.
  • Security fixes from a full review of the code, and the new security page.
  • A status page and a health check that tests RAMen every few minutes.
  • The thank-you page after you buy shows your license key with a Copy button.
  • Download your memory: everything RAMen keeps for you, as text or JSON, from Settings on your RAMen page.
  • A new key bought with the same email asks once for the key you used before, then opens the same memory. One email can be shared, so a memory opens only for someone who has its key.
  • Easier to use with a keyboard and a screen reader, clearer colors in light mode, bigger buttons on phones, and less motion for people who turn it off. Deleting a note offers Undo.

13 November 2026

  • On my devices: keep your memory on your own computer, with Claude and ChatGPT still able to use it (Pro and the trial).
  • Private memory for local models: the AI apps on your computer share one memory that never leaves it.
  • Any length of chat: a very long saved chat keeps its older details in parts that RAMen still searches.
  • Your saved chats open as a page on your RAMen page: where it stands, to-dos to tick off, decisions, facts and drafts with Copy.
  • A calmer sign-in page with Try again when a key does not work.
  • RAMen for Chrome 1.3.1 in the Chrome Web Store.

12 November 2026

  • The RAMen connector for other AI apps is open source (MIT): github.com/MatthewLeibel/ramen-mcp.
  • The RAMen community on Discord.

11 November 2026

  • RAMen Free, RAMen Pro ($1.99 a month with a 14-day trial) and RAMen Lifetime.
  • Must-know facts: up to 12 things every AI keeps in mind first, like an allergy or a medicine.
  • Your profile card: how you like answers, read by every AI app with RAMen.
  • RAMen in ChatGPT follows OpenAI's rules: it saves only what you say or ask it to keep.
  • RAMen Desktop 1.4.2: up to 70% fewer tokens in long chats with local models.
