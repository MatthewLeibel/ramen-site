Every RAMen update, newest first.

17 November 2026

  • RAMen Cloud AI has three plans with credits each month: Light ($9.99, 1,500 credits), Daily ($24.99, 4,500) and All day ($39.99, 7,500). Run out? Buy more credits in Bowl's settings, any amount from $2. Bought credits never expire.
  • Bowl's settings show how many credits are left, with a bar, and what things use: a briefing about 110 credits, a question about 9.
  • Delete everything on your RAMen page now also deletes everything Bowl worked out (today's briefing, your profile, the week's research), and Download your memory includes it.
  • Bowl understands more ways of saying when: "in April", "before year end", "next year", "for the holidays", "this summer", and "before the tasting" when the tasting has a day.
  • Ask Bowl names the cards it talks about and points at them on the board. "What's due this week?" answers with what is due, and when nothing has a date, with what your plan finishes in those days.
  • Quick add shows days and times the way the rest of Bowl does ("due Thu 19 Nov, 30 minutes"). From all projects together, it adds to the project your words are about and says which.
  • When your to-dos have no date yet, Today says so, how to start the outlook, and how much of them Bowl planned this week.
  • RAMenScope's news from the web on your topics comes through again (it often came back empty), always with its source, and never from pages more than a year old.
  • Bowl reads "end of next month", "end of next week" and quarters ("by the end of Q1", "Q2 2027", "next quarter"). What-ifs and briefings write days the way the rest of Bowl does ("Tue 1 Dec").
  • The support page answers questions about RAMen Bowl, its AI, and RAMen Cloud AI's plans and credits.
  • The privacy policy, terms and refund policy now cover RAMen Bowl and RAMen Cloud AI.
