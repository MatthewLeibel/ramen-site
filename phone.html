RAMen on your phone.

Private AI that runs on the phone itself and remembers every chat. No account, no app store and no cloud: scan the code with your phone's camera to open it.

Open RAMen

getramenai.com/app

  1. STEP 01

    Scan the code

    Point your phone's camera at it. RAMen opens in the browser, on iPhone and Android.

  2. STEP 02

    Download the model once

    Qwen3 0.6B, 397 MB, kept on the phone. After that RAMen works offline.

  3. STEP 03

    Keep it on your Home Screen

    On iPhone, tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, choose Install in Chrome. Every chat comes back with its memory.
