The short version

RAMen runs on your computer. Your prompts, files, conversations and models stay there, unless you choose a cloud model with your own key (RAMen Cloud, below) or turn on RAMen Online, which keeps your RAMen Memory on our servers so Claude and ChatGPT can use it (below). We never read, sell or share it, and we never use it to train any model.

What RAMen sends, and when

  • Paid plans only: when RAMen starts, it checks your license key with our store provider, Polar (polar.sh). It sends the key, our store's identifier and an anonymous machine label: the platform (for example "macOS-arm64") and a short code derived from the machine, never its name. Polar sees your internet address, as with any connection.
  • Model downloads: when ramen start downloads a model, it connects to Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which sees your internet address and the file requested. If you set a Hugging Face token, it is sent to Hugging Face only.
  • RAMen Cloud, only if you set it up: when you save your own Claude or OpenAI API key with ramen cloud key, the conversations your apps have with that company's models go to that company (Anthropic or OpenAI) with your key, as they would from the app itself, under that company's own terms and privacy policy. With ramen cloud memory on, the notes RAMen Sleep wrote that match each question go along with it; that is off unless you turn it on. Your key is kept on your computer, in a file only your account can read, and is sent only to its company. Requests for any other model stay on your computer, and with no key saved nothing goes to a cloud.
  • RAMen Memory in another app: when an app such as Claude Desktop or Claude Code uses RAMen Memory (ramen mcp), the short answers it asks for go to that app, and from there to the company behind it.
  • Nothing else. RAMen has no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.

RAMen Online

RAMen Online is an optional part of RAMen Pro that lets Claude (claude.ai, the desktop and phone apps) and ChatGPT use your RAMen Memory, also when your computer is off. It runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects), which stores data encrypted at rest.

  • What it keeps: the things your connected apps save to your memory (when you tell them a lasting fact or ask them to remember something), and, if you link a computer with ramen online, the study notes RAMen Sleep wrote and your own sentences from your conversations, with the time of the conversation each came from. It also keeps the name of each linked computer's platform and a short code for it, and which apps you connected. It does not keep your conversations themselves: Claude and ChatGPT send it only what they save and what they look up.
  • Signing in: you sign in with your RAMen Pro license key. We check it with Polar (polar.sh) and keep only a one-way hash of it, which ties your memory to your key. The apps you connect get access tokens that we keep only as one-way hashes. To limit sign-in attempts, it remembers the internet address a sign-in came from for up to an hour.
  • Who sees it: the apps you connected, when they look something up for you, and the computers you linked. Each lookup's question and answer pass through Cloudflare. We do not read your memory, keep logs of what is saved or looked up, sell it, share it, or use it to train any model.
  • Deleting it: ask your AI to forget something, or run ramen online --forget to delete everything online and disconnect every app. ramen online --off unlinks a computer and keeps the memory for your apps. Email support@getramenai.com to have it deleted for you.
  • Your memory on your own computer is not affected by any of this.

Our website

getramenai.com counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, so we know how many people read each page. It sets no cookies and does not follow you across other sites. Cloudflare sees your internet address and browser type, as any website does, and gives us only totals such as page views, referring sites and countries. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which also sees your internet address.

What we receive when you buy

Polar processes your purchase as the reseller. We receive your name, email address, country and what you bought, so we can support you and send license and account messages. We do not receive your full card details. See Polar's privacy policy for how Polar handles payment data.

Email

If you write to support@getramenai.com, we keep the conversation to help you. If you ask for launch updates on our website, we keep your email address in our sign-up list (stored with Cloudflare, with the date and the country of the request) and email you about RAMen. Reply "stop" or write to support@getramenai.com at any time and we remove you.

Affiliate program

If you apply to our affiliate program, we keep what you send in the form (your name, email address, links, the code you would like and your note, with the date and the country of the request), stored with Cloudflare, to answer you and run the program. For each affiliate we keep their code and the sales made with it, read from Polar: the date and amount of each sale, never who bought. An affiliate's private page shows only that. Payout details you send us are used only to pay you.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it: support@getramenai.com. Some purchase records must be kept for tax law.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell customers by email.
