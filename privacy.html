The short version

RAMen Desktop runs on your computer: your prompts, files, conversations and models stay there, unless you link it to RAMen Online or choose a cloud model with your own key (both below). RAMen Online keeps your RAMen Memory and your saved chats on our servers, so you can use them in Claude, ChatGPT and the RAMen add-on for Chrome (below). With On my devices (RAMen Pro), they live on your own computer instead, and RAMen Online only passes your apps' requests to it (below). In the add-on, Detail Lock keeps its lists in your browser; they reach us only inside the chats the add-on saves to your RAMen (every 15 messages while "Save long chats as they go" is on, which you can turn off).

We never read, sell or share what you keep with RAMen, and we never use it to train any model.

What RAMen Desktop sends, and when

  • Your license: when RAMen starts with a license key, it checks the key with our store provider, Polar (polar.sh). It sends the key, our store's identifier and an anonymous machine label: the platform (for example "macOS-arm64") and a short code derived from the machine, never its name. Polar sees your internet address, as with any connection.
  • Model downloads: when ramen start downloads a model, it connects to Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which sees your internet address and the file requested. If you set a Hugging Face token, it is sent to Hugging Face only.
  • RAMen Online, only if you link this computer: after ramen online, RAMen sends the notes RAMen Sleep wrote and your own sentences from your conversations to your RAMen Memory online, and brings down what your apps saved there. It does this after each ramen sleep and every 15 minutes while it runs, until you unlink the computer with ramen online --off.
  • RAMen Cloud, only if you set it up: when you save your own Claude or OpenAI API key with ramen cloud key, the conversations your apps have with that company's models go to that company (Anthropic or OpenAI) with your key, as they would from the app itself, under that company's own terms and privacy policy. With ramen cloud memory on, the notes RAMen Sleep wrote that match each question go along with it; that is off unless you turn it on. Your key is kept on your computer, in a file only your account can read, and is sent only to its company. Requests for any other model stay on your computer, and with no key saved nothing goes to a cloud.
  • RAMen Memory in another app: when an app such as Claude Desktop or Claude Code uses RAMen Memory (ramen mcp), the short answers it asks for go to that app, and from there to the company behind it.
  • Nothing else. RAMen Desktop has no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.

RAMen Online

RAMen Online is the part of RAMen behind the RAMen connector and the RAMen add-on for Chrome, on every plan (RAMen Free with its limits): it lets Claude (claude.ai, the desktop and phone apps, Claude Code), ChatGPT and the AI websites in Chrome use your RAMen Memory, also when your computer is off. It runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects), which stores data encrypted at rest.

  • What it keeps: the notes your connected apps save to your memory (when you tell them a lasting fact or ask them to remember something). Each note keeps the date, the app, and whether you said it or the AI guessed, and the words from your chat it came from when the app sends them along. A guess waits for your OK before any app uses it. It also keeps the chats you or your AI save to continue later (their goal, current values, facts, decisions, open items and drafts, and the project you put them in), including the checkpoints your AI saves in a long chat, about every 15 messages, while automatic checkpoints are on (they are on at first; turn them off in Settings on your RAMen page); your profile card (how you like answers); your settings; the name you give on your RAMen page; and, if you link a computer with ramen online, the study notes RAMen Sleep wrote and your own sentences from your conversations, with the time of the conversation each came from. It also keeps the name of each linked computer's platform and a short code for it, and which apps you connected. For the plans, it keeps which plan your key is (RAMen Free or Pro), when a request last came with a Pro key and the first time one came with a Free key, and how many chats were saved this month, so it can keep RAMen Free's limits. It does not keep whole conversations: your apps send it only what they save and what they look up. In ChatGPT and Codex, RAMen keeps only what you say or ask it to keep, never an AI's guess about you, and saves a chat only when you ask.
  • Sensitive topics: notes about topics such as health or money are kept like any other, marked with their topic. You can turn any topic off under Advanced settings on your RAMen page, and RAMen then does not save new notes about it.
  • Signing in: you sign in with your RAMen license key (RAMen Free, Pro or Lifetime). We check it with Polar (polar.sh) and keep a one-way hash of it, which ties your memory to your key. Polar's answer also names the customer the key was bought by, and we keep a one-way hash of that Polar customer ID, so keys bought with the same email (for example RAMen Free, then Pro) share one memory; we never keep the customer ID itself. So that RAMen stops when your plan or trial ends, we also keep your key encrypted with each app's connection and each linked computer's link, where it can only be read when that app renews its access, and your RAMen page keeps it encrypted in its sign-in cookie in your browser; RAMen uses it only to check your key with Polar again from time to time. The apps you connect get access tokens that we keep only as one-way hashes. To limit sign-in attempts, it remembers the internet address a sign-in came from for up to an hour.
  • Who sees it: the apps you connected, when they look something up for you, and the computers you linked. Each lookup's question and answer pass through Cloudflare. We do not read your memory, keep logs of what is saved or looked up, sell it, share it, or use it to train any model.
  • Deleting it: on your RAMen page you can delete any note or saved chat, change or clear your profile card, and disconnect any app; RAMen's panel in the chat can delete notes and saved chats too. You can also ask your AI to forget something. To delete everything at once (your notes, saved chats, profile card and settings) and disconnect every app, run ramen online --forget on a computer you linked with RAMen Desktop, or email support@getramenai.com and we delete everything for you. ramen online --off unlinks a computer and keeps the memory for your apps.
  • How long it is kept: your notes, saved chats, profile card and settings stay until you delete them. What you delete is gone from your memory at once and from our hosting provider's backups (Cloudflare) within 30 days. When you ask us by email, we delete everything within 30 days. The internet address of a sign-in is kept for up to an hour, and the RAMen page's sign-in cookie for up to 30 days.
  • When your plan ends: if you cancel, or a trial ends without a payment, your connected apps stop reaching RAMen Online within about a day and a half, and signing in again, on your RAMen page, in a new app or on a new computer, needs an active key. With a RAMen Free key from the same email you keep your memory and use it within RAMen Free's limits; about 3 days after the last request with a Pro key, your memory follows RAMen Free's limits. Your RAMen Online data is not deleted on its own: delete it yourself as above, or email support@getramenai.com at any time and we delete everything.
  • Your memory on your own computer is not affected by any of this.

On my devices

With On my devices (RAMen Pro and the 14-day trial, not RAMen Free), your RAMen Memory lives on a computer of yours instead of on our servers. RAMen on your computer (ramen-vault) keeps it in one file there, in a folder only your account on that computer can read.

  • Moving it: when you move your memory to your computer, all of it (notes, must-know facts, saved chats, your profile card and settings) is copied there, and the copy on our servers is deleted only after your computer confirms it has all of it. Moving it back to RAMen Cloud works the same way the other way round.
  • What passes through: while your memory is on your computer, each request from an app you connected (Claude, ChatGPT, the RAMen add-on for Chrome, your RAMen page) goes to RAMen Online, which passes it to your computer over an encrypted connection your computer opened, and passes the answer back. RAMen Online does not store these requests or answers. While the computer is off, your apps are told so, and nothing is saved anywhere else. Desktop apps on that computer (such as Claude Desktop or Claude Code) read the file directly, without RAMen Online.
  • What RAMen Online keeps for it: which of your linked computers holds your memory (a random code, and the name your computer gives itself, for example "Dana's MacBook (Mac)"), since when, and whether it is connected now; and, as for every linked computer, your license key encrypted with its link, to check your plan from time to time.
  • When a plan ends: your memory stays on your computer, and the computer can still move it back to RAMen Cloud, to use it with RAMen Free.
  • Your computer, your copy: we cannot see or recover what is on your computer. If the computer is lost before you moved the memory back, the memory is lost with it, so keep the computer backed up (for example with Time Machine) or keep a copy with ramen-vault export.
  • Deleting it: Delete everything on your RAMen page deletes the memory on your computer too when the computer is connected at that moment; ramen-vault forget --yes on the computer deletes it there at any time.

The RAMen add-on for Chrome

The RAMen add-on for Chrome brings Detail Lock, and your RAMen Memory and saved chats, to the AI websites it works on (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, DeepSeek, Mistral, Meta AI, Poe and Qwen). It works while you are signed in to RAMen. Detail Lock comes with RAMen Pro: it reads your messages and the AI's answers on those sites, to find the details you set and to check the answers against them. On RAMen Free, Detail Lock is off, and the add-on reads your messages only to count them and see how long the chat is, so it knows when to add your must-know facts.

  • What it keeps in your browser: in the add-on's own storage, on your computer: for each chat, the details it found and the ones you added, the last check of an answer, the chat's to-dos and about how long the chat is, the start of the chat's first message (as its title and goal), your last message and the AI's last answer (for Fresh start with everything), and short codes for the messages it has already read; your settings; a copy of your must-know facts (the notes you mark as must-know in your RAMen Memory) and of your plan with what RAMen Free counts, asked for again from RAMen every 10 minutes and removed when you sign out; and your RAMen sign-in (the access RAMen's sign-in page gave it, never your license key). The message for a Fresh start waits there only until the new chat opens.
  • Detail Lock stays in your browser: nothing in its lists is sent to RAMen or to anyone else, except inside your own messages and in the automatic saves of a chat to your own RAMen account (both below). The add-on has no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your RAMen account: what you save or search in the add-on goes to your RAMen account in RAMen Online, as with the connector (above): a search (with Add what RAMen knows, the text in your message box), a note you save, a note you mark or unmark as must-know, a chat you save (what the AI writes about it when you ask it to save the chat), the guesses you approve or reject, the notes you forget and the settings you change. On RAMen Pro, every 15 messages, while "Save long chats as they go" is on in your RAMen settings (it is on at first), it also saves the chat to your RAMen account by itself: its goal, its details, its to-dos and the AI's latest answer, under the chat's title. It also sends the name of the AI site you are on, so a note's receipt can name it.
  • What it adds to your messages: when you send a message, it can add your list of details to it, at the times you choose (once the chat is too long for the AI to see it all, which is the setting at first; always; or never). It also adds your must-know facts under your first message in each new chat, and under every message once the chat is too long for the AI to see its start, until you turn that off in the add-on's Memory tab. That message goes to the chat site you are using, as anything you write there does, under that site's own terms and privacy policy. Remind it and Fresh start with everything only put text in the message box: nothing is sent until you press Send.
  • Smarter checks, only if you turn them on: the check runs on Chrome's built-in AI on your computer, or on RAMen Desktop on the same computer, which Detail Lock asks your OK to reach. Nothing leaves your computer.
  • Clearing and deleting: Clear this chat's details, in the add-on's Settings, removes only that chat's details and its last check; the rest of what it keeps for that chat stays until you remove the add-on. Sign out removes your RAMen sign-in from the add-on. Removing the add-on from Chrome deletes everything it kept in your browser.

Our website and the RAMen page

getramenai.com counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, so we know how many people read each page. It sets no cookies and does not follow you across other sites. Cloudflare sees your internet address and browser type, as any website does, and gives us only totals such as page views, referring sites and countries. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which also sees your internet address. If you come from an affiliate's link (getramenai.com/?code=...), the page keeps that code in your browser for the visit only, never in a cookie, and checks it with our affiliate service.

Your RAMen page (ramen-online.matthewsleibel.workers.dev/app) runs on Cloudflare with RAMen Online. Signing in there sets one cookie, ramen_hub, which keeps you signed in for up to 30 days or until you sign out. It holds a signed code for your account and your license key, encrypted so that only RAMen's server can read it, to check your key with Polar again from time to time. The page also remembers a few display choices in your browser, such as light or dark.

Our pages and the RAMen page load the Inter font from Google Fonts, so Google sees your internet address and browser when the font loads, under Google's own privacy policy.

What we receive when you buy

Polar processes your purchase as the reseller, including the card it asks for when you start the trial. We receive your name, email address, country and what you bought, so we can support you and send license and account messages. We do not receive your full card details. See Polar's privacy policy for how Polar handles payment data.

Email

If you write to support@getramenai.com, we keep the conversation to help you. If you ask for news on our website (or to hear when the RAMen add-on is in the Chrome Web Store), we keep your email address in our sign-up list (stored with Cloudflare, with the date, the page and the country of the request) and email you about RAMen. Reply "stop" or write to support@getramenai.com at any time and we remove you.

Affiliate program

If you apply to our affiliate program, we keep what you send in the form (your name, email address, links, the code you would like and your note, with the date and the country of the request), stored with Cloudflare, to answer you and run the program. For each affiliate we keep their code and the sales made with it, read from Polar: the date and amount of each sale, never who bought. An affiliate's private page shows only that. Payout details you send us are used only to pay you.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, including everything in RAMen Online: support@getramenai.com. Some purchase records must be kept for tax law.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell customers by email.
