RAMen Refund Policy

Effective 11 November 2026.

RAMen Pro, monthly or yearly, starts with a free 14-day trial of everything, so you can check RAMen works for you before paying. RAMen Free costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund.

  • Monthly and yearly plans: ask within 14 days of a payment for a full refund of that payment. Cancel any time from the customer portal (https://polar.sh/ramen/portal); you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • RAMen Lifetime: ask within 14 days of buying it for a full refund.
  • Pro for life (sold at launch): you keep it, and you can ask for a full refund within 30 days of buying it.

Ask at support@getramenai.com with the email you bought with. Refunds are paid by Polar to the original payment method. After a refund the plan's Pro parts stop; your memory stays, and a RAMen Free key from the same email keeps it.
