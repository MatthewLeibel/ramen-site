RAMen Security

How your memory is kept safe, and how to tell us about a problem. Updated 14 November 2026.

How your memory is protected

  • Your memory is yours alone. Each person's memory is kept apart from everyone else's, and one person's sign-in never reaches another person's memory. Every release is tested for this.
  • Signing in. Apps connect over HTTPS with OAuth and PKCE, the way Claude and ChatGPT connect to any connector. Your license key and the apps' access tokens are kept only as one-way hashes; the copy of your key used to check your plan is kept encrypted (details in the privacy policy).
  • The RAMen page uses a signed, encrypted sign-in cookie, refuses changes sent from other websites, and loads no scripts from anywhere else.
  • You decide. A guess an AI makes about you waits for your OK. The memory panel's buttons that can delete things are given only to the apps that show the panel to you (Claude, ChatGPT and the RAMen add-on), never to an AI on its own.
  • On your own devices. With On my devices your memory lives on your computer, and RAMen Online only passes requests through and keeps none of it. Local models can have a private memory that never leaves the computer.
  • Delete everything in Settings on your RAMen page deletes your memory, disconnects every app and computer, and signs out every browser.
  • We do not sell your data, share it, or use it to train any model.

How we test

Before every release RAMen passes its automated tests: signing in, every tool, the memory panel, your own computer, the RAMen page in the engines of Chrome, Safari and Firefox, and more than 1,300 odd, broken and hostile inputs (pasted keys with stray characters, HTML and scripts in notes, malformed requests). A full security review of the code was done on 14 November 2026 and its findings were fixed. You can see RAMen's live health on the status page.

Report a security problem

Email support@getramenai.com with "Security" in the subject: what you found and how to see it. Please give us a chance to fix it before telling others. We read every report and reply. Please do not test against other people's memories or accounts.
