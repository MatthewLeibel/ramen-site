Fix it yourself

Most problems have a quick fix. Find what you see below. Is RAMen itself down? The status page says.

Connecting Claude or ChatGPT

Step by step, with everything to copy: RAMen in Claude, RAMen in ChatGPT (paid plans) and other apps. When asked to sign in, paste your RAMen key.

"Could not connect" when you sign in

  • "That license key was not found, or its plan has ended": copy the key again from your Polar email (it starts with RAMEN-); stray quotes or spaces are fine. Plan ended? RAMen Free with the same email keeps your memory.
  • "Too many tries": wait fifteen minutes, then connect again from the app.
  • "Busy right now": new sign-ins are paused for a while. Apps you already connected keep working. Try again in an hour.
  • "Not connected: RAMen Free connects up to 2 apps": disconnect one on your RAMen page (Connections), then connect again.

"You have used RAMen before"

Your email already has a RAMen memory. Paste the key you used before (in your earlier Polar email or your customer portal) to keep it, or start fresh. RAMen asks because families and teams can share an email.

Your AI does not use RAMen

  • Add the one line from the setup page to your app's instructions (Claude: Settings › Your Claude › Instructions; ChatGPT: Custom instructions). It tells your AI to use RAMen.
  • In Claude, check that RAMen is switched on for the chat, in the chat box's tools menu.
  • ChatGPT shows fewer than 7 RAMen tools? It keeps the list from the day you added RAMen. Refresh RAMen in ChatGPT's settings. Your memory stays.
  • You can always ask in plain words: "Check RAMen for my dentist" or "Save this to RAMen".

RAMen did not find something you told it

  • Look on your RAMen page under Notes (search it). If an AI only guessed it, it waits for your OK under Pending.
  • Ask in other words, or name the chat it came from: "What did we decide in the wedding chat?"
  • If you turned a sensitive topic off (Settings, Advanced), RAMen does not save new notes about it.

Going on with a long chat

In the long chat, say "Save this chat to RAMen". In a fresh chat or another AI, say "Continue my RAMen chat" and its name.

"Not saved" on RAMen Free

RAMen Free keeps 50 notes, 3 must-know facts, 3 chat saves a month and 2 apps. You're warned before a limit, and nothing saved is lost. Delete what you don't need, or move to Pro.

Your memory is on your computer, and it is offline

With On my devices, your memory lives on your own computer, so apps reach it only while RAMen runs there. Start it again on that computer: node ~/.ramen/ramen-vault.mjs start.

Your license key

Your key is in the email from Polar, RAMen's store, and in the customer portal at polar.sh/ramen/portal: sign in there with the email you got RAMen with.

RAMen Bowl and its AI

Bowl puts your saved chats and notes on one board, with a timeline, a calendar and a daily briefing. Open it from your RAMen page. It comes with RAMen Pro and the trial; RAMen Free shows the Board and the Timeline of your latest project. In Bowl's settings you pick its AI: Automatic, free AI on your phone or computer or in Chrome, RAMen Cloud AI, your own key, or no AI (RAMen rules). If Bowl says it uses its own rules, the AI you picked is off, out of credits, or can't run on this device.

RAMen Cloud AI's plans and credits

RAMen Cloud AI is Claude by Anthropic, reading for Bowl. A plan comes with credits each month: Light $9.99 (1,500 credits), Daily $24.99 (4,500) or All day $39.99 (7,500). A briefing uses about 110 credits, a question about 9, reading a chat about 3. Bowl's settings show how many are left. Run out? Buy more there, any amount from $2 (100 credits for each $1). Bought credits never expire and are used after your plan's. Change or cancel a plan in the customer portal at polar.sh/ramen/portal. Within 14 days of a payment you can ask for a refund of the credits you haven't used (refund policy).
