4. Your data

RAMen Desktop runs on your computers, and your prompts, files and conversations in it stay there unless you link it to RAMen Online or turn on RAMen Cloud. To check a license key, RAMen Desktop sends the key, our store's identifier and an anonymous machine label (the platform and a short code derived from the machine, never its name) to our store provider, Polar. See the Privacy Policy.

RAMen Online keeps your RAMen Memory and saved chats on our servers so the apps you connect (such as Claude, ChatGPT and the RAMen add-on for Chrome) can use them. It is part of every plan, within RAMen Free's limits on RAMen Free. You are responsible for what you and your apps save to it; we may suspend access that abuses the service or puts it at risk. You can delete what it keeps at any time (on your RAMen page, with ramen online --forget on a linked computer, or by asking us), and after a plan ends it stays until you delete it or ask us to, as the Privacy Policy says. When RAMen Cloud sends your conversations to Anthropic or OpenAI with your own API key, their terms and policies apply to that use. See the Privacy Policy.

With On my devices (RAMen Pro and the trial), your RAMen Memory and saved chats are kept in one file on a computer of yours instead of on our servers, and RAMen Online passes each request from your apps to that computer and the answer back without keeping it. That computer is then the only place your memory is: you are responsible for it and its backups, and we cannot see or recover what is on it. If a paid plan ends, the computer can still move the memory back to RAMen Cloud, to use it with RAMen Free. A private memory for local models is kept only in a file on your computer and is never sent to us, unless you share part of it yourself; you are responsible for it and its backups.
