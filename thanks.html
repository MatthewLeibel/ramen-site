Get started in four steps

  1. STEP 01

    Find your license key

    It is in the email from Polar (our store) and in your customer portal. It starts with RAMEN-.

    We emailed it to you too. Keep it private: it opens your memory.

  2. STEP 02

    Add RAMen to Claude or ChatGPT

    In Claude: Settings › Customize › Connectors › Add custom connector. Name it RAMen, paste this address and sign in with your key:

    https://ramen-online.matthewsleibel.workers.dev/mcp

    In ChatGPT, on a paid plan: Plugins › Add custom MCP server, with the same address. Then add the one line from the setup to your instructions.

  3. STEP 03

    The RAMen add-on for Chrome

    For Detail Lock, and RAMen on ChatGPT, Gemini and the other AI websites it works on. Add to Chrome

  4. STEP 04

    RAMen Desktop, if you use AI on your computer

    Install it (Mac and Linux, in Terminal):

    curl -fsSL https://getramenai.com/install.sh | bash

    Windows, in PowerShell:

    irm https://getramenai.com/install.ps1 | iex

    Then switch on your plan and start:

    ramen activate YOUR-KEY ramen start

    A key works on up to 3 computers. To move it: ramen deactivate.
