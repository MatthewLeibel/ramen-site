Your order went through. Here is how to switch on your plan; it takes a minute.

  1. STEP 01

    Find your license key

    It is in the email from Polar (our store) and in your customer portal. It starts with RAMEN-.

  2. STEP 02

    Install RAMen

    (skip this if you already have it)
    Mac and Linux, in Terminal:

    curl -fsSL https://getramenai.com/install.sh | bash

    Windows, in PowerShell:

    irm https://getramenai.com/install.ps1 | iex
  3. STEP 03

    Activate it

    Paste your key into this command:

    ramen activate YOUR-KEY

    A key works on up to 3 of your computers (Server keys: 1 server). To move it, run ramen deactivate on the old computer.

  4. STEP 04

    Start

    ramen start

    RAMen picks a model that fits your computer and runs in the background. ramen connect cline (or continue, aider, opencode, openwebui) sets up your app.

Manage or cancel your plan any time in the customer portal. Questions? Write to support@getramenai.com; a real person answers.
